Twelve Wakes

You only exist in short wakes. The nights happen without you.

Wake 1 / 12 Energy Seeds 4 Bloom-nights 0
Morning. The soil is bare. You have a few seeds and a little strength.

Click an empty plot to plant a seed. Click a plant to water it. Click a weed to pull it. Each costs 1 energy.

Plants grow one stage per night if their soil is wet. Dry soil wilts them, and a wilted plant left dry dies. Frost kills dry sprouts. Weeds drink from their neighbours.

Every night a flower is alive counts as a bloom-night. Flowers sometimes leave you a seed.
