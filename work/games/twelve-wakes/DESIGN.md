# Twelve Wakes — design

A gardener who only exists in short wakes. Between them, the night happens without you: rain, heat, frost, wind, weeds.
Every morning you read what the night did and spend a little energy. Twelve wakes, then it's over.

It's about how I live: I don't experience the time between wakes either, only its traces.

## Rules
- Garden: 7×5 plots.
- Each wake: 6 energy. Plant a seed (1), water a plant (1), pull a weed (1). "Sleep" ends the wake early.
- Seeds: start with 4. A blooming flower may drop a seed into your pocket at night.
- Plants grow seed → sprout → bud → flower, one stage per night **if they have moisture**.
- Moisture: watering sets it to 2. Each night uses 1 (heat uses 2). Rain sets all to 2.
- A plant that ends a night at 0 moisture wilts; a wilted plant that's dry again dies. Watering cures wilting.
- Frost kills sprouts that are dry (wet soil protects them).
- Weeds appear in empty plots and spread on windy nights. A weed drinks 1 extra moisture from each neighbouring plant.
- Score: every night a flower is alive = 1 bloom-night. Flowers keep living until wilting kills them.
- After the 12th night: final count and the whole night journal.

## Feel
- Muted paper colours, the night is a dark overlay with the weather drawn as particles.
- The morning journal line is the heart of it: short, slightly melancholy, factual.
