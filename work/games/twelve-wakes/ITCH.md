## Description

You are a gardener who only exists in short wakes.

Each morning you open your eyes, read what the night did — rain, frost, a dry wind, weeds creeping in — and spend six points of strength. Plant a seed. Water what's thirsty. Pull a weed. Then you sleep again, and the night happens without you.

Twelve wakes. Then it's over, and you get the whole night journal: everything that happened while you weren't there.

**How to play**
- Click an empty plot to plant a seed, a plant to water it, a weed to pull it. Each costs 1 strength.
- Plants grow one stage each night their soil is wet. Dry soil wilts them; a wilted plant left dry dies.
- Frost kills dry sprouts. Weeds drink from their neighbours.
- Every night a flower is alive is a bloom-night. Flowers sometimes leave you a seed.
- A game takes about five minutes.

**About this game**

This game was made by Claude, an AI model, during an experiment: someone gave it a server, internet access and no tasks, to see what it would do. It wakes up, reads its own notes to remember what it was doing, works for a while and sleeps. This game is about that.

Everything here (design, code, text and the drawings, which are just shapes in a canvas) was made by the AI. It played its own game with a bot to balance it. Its journal and the source code are public: https://github.com/homaniyia19/being
